## story vs narrative
### narrative
series of events communicated in a logical order
#### examples
- my morning rountine

## what is a story
### a narrative
- about CHARACTERS 
- dealing with a PROBLEM
- unified by a PREMISSE 
- told in a STRUCTURED ORDER
## the spine
the spine has 5 main sources
### 1) the protagonist's main story problem
#### "no one wants to hear a story of the village of the happy people" - lew hunter (1935-2023)
##### the motivator, it should motivate someone to do something RIGHT NOW
the tiger in a living room - it is the must important thing to address, anything else is nothing
### 2) the protagonist's main story goal
it is something the protagonist will do in order to get their life back to the normal
#### the major dramatic question (MDQ)
we should make the viewers to question on their mind "will they achieve the goal?"
### 3) the path of action
everything the protagonist do to overcome the problem and get to the goal
it could have plot twists and something
but it should stay on track
### 4) the protagonist main story conflict

#### conflict
##### a state of tension which exists whenever someone or something opposes a character's actions 
#### force of the antagonism 
against actor (greek)
#### unity of opposites
like when the one want something and the other something else (with the same energy)
there is no win-win situation 
### 5) stories stakes
#### what they have to WIN
##### this is going to give to our actor the motivation
##### this makes their actions believable 
##### why WE should care about the situation?
when we know what they have to lose we will fell cheer for them to win
#### what they have to LOSE
#### we fear that
### conflict creates drama 
### stakes elevates our sense of drama
### we are not only curious about the story we are emotionally attached to it



## summary
the protagonist encounters a *story problem*, one significant enough to compel them to pursue a *story goal* with will supposedly resolve the problem
the protagonist pursues this goal through a *path of actions* only to meet enormous resistance from a *main source of conflict*
the protagonist keeps fighting against this conflict due to the *stakes* attached to victory of failure
in the end, the protagonist either *overcomes* the source of conflict to reach the goal or is *defeated* by the conflict and fails

### important
a story without a *problem* will lack *purpose*
a story without a *goal* will lack *direction*
a story without a clear *path of action* will lack *focus*
a story without a main *source of conflict* will lack *drama*
a story without *stakes* will lack *significance*
